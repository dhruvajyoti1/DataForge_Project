interruption test.

==================================================
INTERRUPTION OBSERVATION
==================================================

During interruption tests, the system detected stale tasks
and printed:

"Task stale due to interruption! Fencing result."

This indicates that interrupted tasks were detected and fenced
instead of being allowed to return stale results.

==================================================
BASELINE CONCLUSION
==================================================

EchoAssist provides low-latency conversational responses,
while lookup-based responses naturally take longer.

The interruption mechanism successfully detects and fences
stale tasks during testing.
